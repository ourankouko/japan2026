Girls' Trip · Autumn Foliage

Japan, Slowly

Oct 2–13, 2026 11 nights Land NRT 8:50am, Depart NRT 4:50pm ANA
Narita→ Aomori (5N)→ Sendai + Matsushima (2N)→ Tokyo (4N)→ Narita

Why this order: Aomori first, since you're travelling with luggage on arrival day regardless — no point checking into a Tokyo hotel first just to leave again. Sendai sits directly on the line back down, so it costs nothing extra to break the journey there instead of racing straight through. Tokyo last for shopping and easy departure.

JR EAST PASS (5-day, ¥35,000 pp): activate at Narita on Oct 2. Covers Narita Express, the big Tokyo→Aomori Shinkansen, and local JR trains in Aomori through Oct 6. The Aomori→Sendai leg on Oct 7 falls just outside the window — buy that one individually, it's cheap.

Rental car in Aomori: book via Toyota Rent-A-Car or JR Rent-A-Car before you leave Singapore. International Driving Permit needed for whoever drives — get it from Singapore AA, ~20 minutes, ~SGD 20.

The one date-sensitive booking: Sushi Kadowaki (Ginza) is closed Sundays — it's placed on the Karuizawa day (Saturday) for this reason. Call ahead as soon as dates are confirmed; it books out weeks in advance.

Oct 3Saturday
Hirosaki Castle + Sannai Maruyama
Original castle keep · UNESCO Jomon site · apple country — placed on the weekend since both are flat, spacious sites that absorb crowds well
🚗 45-min drive to Hirosaki, or JR Limited Express (35 min) if you'd rather not drive yet. Return via Sannai Maruyama en route back to Aomori City.
9:00 AM
Hirosaki Castle & Park
One of only 12 original castle keeps in Japan — not a reconstruction. The park is enormous, multiple moats. Early October: maples starting to turn, castle reflected in still water. Allow 2 hours minimum, flat walking throughout.
Castle · HistoryFoliage
Midday
Apple cider tasting + lunch
Hirosaki is Japan's apple capital. Try both the cloudy fresh juice and a proper craft cider — completely different products. Lunch near the Nakamachi samurai district.
Food · Regional
Afternoon
Sannai Maruyama Archaeological Site
5,000-year-old Jomon settlement — reconstructed pit dwellings, massive 6-pillar wooden structures, active excavation pits. UNESCO World Heritage. Flat site, 90-minute easy walk. The scale genuinely surprises people.
UNESCO · History

Oct 5Monday
Oirase Gorge + Lake Towada
Best single day of scenery in the entire trip — placed on a weekday deliberately, this is the one worth a quiet gorge 🍁
🚗 Drive from Aomori City to Oirase Gorge entrance: ~1h 15min. Arrive before 8am for a near-empty gorge on a Monday.
8:00 AM
Oirase Gorge walk
14km gorge with the river running its entire length — waterfalls every few hundred metres, mossy boulders, early-turning maple and beech canopy. Park at Nenokuchi and walk downhill, or drive in and walk sections flexibly. Proper walking shoes needed — the path is maintained gravel but wet near the falls. Allow 2–3 hours for a good section.
Gorge · WaterfallsEasy–moderate walk
Midday
Lake Towada + Art Center
Caldera lake at the gorge's head. Crystal clear water, complete quiet on a weekday morning. Outdoor sculptures at the Art Center, 45 minutes if wanted. Lunch by the lake.
Lake · ViewsArt
Afternoon
Drive back · Yakeyama viewpoint
Elevated views across the gorge on the return, 10-minute stop. Back in Aomori City by late afternoon.
Views
Oct 6Tuesday
Hakkoda Mountains
Ropeway · summit plateau walk · Sukayu Onsen — also a weekday, ropeway queues are far worse on weekends
🚗 30-min drive from Aomori City. Check the weather forecast the night before — the ropeway closes in strong wind.
8:00 AM
Hakkoda Ropeway to summit (1,324m)
3-minute gondola. Bring a warm layer — noticeably colder at altitude. Foliage spreading below, open alpine terrain above.
Ropeway · Views
Mid AM
Summit plateau loop walk
60–90 minutes at a relaxed pace, mostly flat with gentle undulation. Good walking shoes are enough — no trekking gear needed. This is the easy scenic hike of the trip.
Easy alpine hike
Midday
Jigoku-numa (Hell Pond)
Thermal crater lake near the ropeway base — eerie blue-green water ringed by autumn forest. 15-minute flat loop, don't skip it.
Crater lake
Afternoon
Sukayu Onsen
Historic 1,000-person hinoki wood communal bath. Milky white sulphur water — one of Japan's original designated hot spring resorts. Separate facilities available if the traditional mixed-gender bath isn't your preference.
Onsen
Tonight: return the rental car to Shin-Aomori Station and forward luggage to your Tokyo hotel via Yamato takuhaibin (¥2,000–2,500/bag, arrives next day). Pack only what you need for the Sendai leg.

Dinner — gyutan (beef tongue)
Sumiyaki Gyutan Higashiyama · Sendai city centre~¥2,500–4,000 pp
Charcoal-grilled beef tongue is Sendai's defining dish — non-negotiable. Comes with oxtail soup and barley rice as a set. Small restaurant, tatami seating.
Can get a queue on weekend evenings — you're going on a Wednesday so this shouldn't be an issue, but arrive close to opening (11:30am/5pm) to be safe.
Oct 8Thursday
Matsushima Bay + Sendai History
One of Japan's three officially designated scenic views
🚆 Sendai Station → Matsushima-Kaigan: JR Senseki Line (local), ~40 min, ¥410 one-way.
8:30 AM
Senseki Line to Matsushima-Kaigan
Local train, departs roughly every 20 minutes. Matsushima-Kaigan Station puts you right at the bay.
Local train
9:30 AM
Matsushima Bay cruise
Sightseeing boat through roughly 260 small pine-covered islands. About 50 minutes, ¥1,500–1,800. Book online ahead to skip the queue — the standard deck is fine, don't pay extra for the upper deck.
Bay cruise · Islands
Late AM
Zuigan-ji Temple
Zen temple founded in the 9th century, rebuilt in 1609 by Date Masamune himself. Cedar-lined approach, mossy stone paths, genuinely serene. 45–60 minute visit.
Zen temple · History
Midday
Lunch in Matsushima — oysters or anago
Matsushima Bay is known for oysters (best in cooler months — early October is right on the edge, ask what's fresh) and grilled conger eel. Plenty of casual seafood spots near the pier.
Seafood · Regional
Afternoon
Return to Sendai · Zuihoden Mausoleum
Back on the Senseki Line (~40 min). Zuihoden is Date Masamune's mausoleum — ornate architecture set in cedar forest, reached via a steep uphill stair walk, or the Loople Sendai tourist bus (¥260/ride, ¥960 day pass).
Mausoleum · History
Dinner
Gyutan Cuisine Kaku Brandome · Sendai city centre~¥3,000–5,000 pp
A second, different take on gyutan — beef tongue tataki and premium wagyu tongue sets stand out here. Worth comparing against last night, or swap for something else if you've had your fill of beef tongue.
Tonight: nothing to arrange, no car in this leg — just an early night before Tokyo tomorrow.
