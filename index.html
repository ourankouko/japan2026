Girls' Trip · Autumn Foliage

Japan, Slowly

Oct 2–13, 2026 11 nights Land NRT 8:50am, Depart NRT 4:50pm ANA
Narita→ Aomori (5N)→ Sendai + Matsushima (2N)→ Tokyo (4N)→ Narita

Why this order: Aomori first, since you're travelling with luggage on arrival day regardless — no point checking into a Tokyo hotel first just to leave again. Sendai sits directly on the line back down, so it costs nothing extra to break the journey there instead of racing straight through. Tokyo last for shopping and easy departure.

JR EAST PASS (10-day, ¥50,000 pp — already purchased): activate at Narita on Oct 2. The new unified pass (launched March 2026) covers all JR East lines — Tohoku, Koshinetsu, and Kanto — in one ticket, which means it now covers almost this entire trip: Narita Express, Tokyo↔Aomori, Hirosaki limited express, Aomori↔Sendai, the Senseki Line to Matsushima, Sendai↔Tokyo, Tokyo↔Karuizawa, and Tokyo↔Kamakura are all included. Activated Oct 2, the 10 consecutive days run through Oct 11 — only the final Narita Express on Oct 13 falls outside the window and needs a separate ticket (~¥3,250 pp). This is a big upgrade from booking individual tickets — no more per-segment purchases, and reserved seats on the busy Hayabusa trains come free with the pass.

Rental car in Aomori: booked for Oct 4, 10am – Oct 6, 7pm (3 days) — pick up at Shin-Aomori Station. This covers the Hachinohe day trip, Oirase Gorge, and Hakkoda; Hirosaki on Oct 3 is by train since the car isn't active yet. International Driving Permit needed for whoever drives — get it from Singapore AA, ~20 minutes, ~SGD 20.

The one date-sensitive booking: Sushi Kadowaki (Ginza) is closed Sundays — it's placed on the Karuizawa day (Saturday) for this reason. Call ahead as soon as dates are confirmed; it books out weeks in advance.

Tokyo Shopping — Ueno + Akihabara
Pancake queue · motorcycle gear · sukiyaki lunch · figures · Omotesando evening wind-down
Honest heads-up: this is a heavy food day — a soufflé pancake stack in the morning followed by a full sukiyaki lunch a couple hours later. Consider sharing the pancakes between the two of you, or treating Imahan as a lighter lunch set, so you're not overly full for an afternoon of walking Akihabara.
7:00 AM
Benizuru Pancake — join the ticket queue
Nishi-Asakusa, near Tawaramachi Station (Ginza Line) — about 10 min from Ueno by train. This is a genuinely divisive spot: some find the fluffy rice-flour soufflé pancakes worth it, others call it an overhyped tourist queue with average pancakes and no locals in sight. The system: arrive early to join the line for a same-day numbered ticket, pay a small deposit, and you're given a timeslot — which could be within the hour or several hours later depending on the day. You then leave and come back at your assigned time. Cash only for the deposit. Given the wait-and-return system, budget flexibility into this morning rather than expecting a quick sit-down breakfast.
